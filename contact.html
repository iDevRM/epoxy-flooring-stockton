What Happens When You Call?

1. Two minutes on the phone

We ask the size of the slab, its age, whether anything has been coated on it before, and what the stains and cracks look like. That is usually enough to give you a ballpark before anyone drives out.

2. We come look at it

The on-site visit takes about 20 minutes. We measure, drop water on the concrete to see how it absorbs, check for a previous sealer, and look at the cracks up close.

3. A written number, on the spot

You get the price before we leave, broken into prep and coating so you can see where the money goes. No pressure and no follow-up calls you did not ask for.
